# SurePlay

SurePlay is an AI decision and action system for people navigating high-uncertainty stages of life.

It is designed for moments when people do not lack information, but lack clarity: when they are trying to understand where they are, what direction is worth pursuing, and what they should do next.

SurePlay does not treat life as a fixed route to be optimized. It helps users turn fragmented inputs, internal confusion, and ongoing life signals into clearer judgment, narrower direction, and real action.

In simple terms, SurePlay helps transform:

- volatility into velocity
- uncertainty into understanding
- complexity into clarity
- ambiguity into agility

## Who SurePlay Is For

SurePlay is built for people who are thinking seriously about their future and making decisions under uncertainty. Early-fit users include:

- university and graduate students
- young professionals in transition
- international students and early-career migrants
- early-stage founders and independent builders
- reflective users who want more than generic AI answers

SurePlay is most useful when a user is facing open-ended questions such as:

- Should I continue on my current path or change direction?
- Why do I feel stuck even though I am trying hard?
- How do I make sense of a confusing stage of life?
- What is the next real move, not just another idea?

## How SurePlay Works

SurePlay is not a notes app, not a one-off AI chat tool, and not just a productivity tracker.

Its core workflow is:

**Input -> Understanding -> Direction -> Action**

That means SurePlay helps users:

1. bring in real-life signals such as text, files, voice, and ongoing reflections
2. understand their current state, pressure points, blockers, and opportunities
3. narrow scattered possibilities into a smaller number of directions worth pursuing
4. turn those directions into practical next-step actions

## Current Product Surfaces

The current MVP includes:

- a public landing and account-entry flow
- the `Haze Map` workspace after sign-in
- raw data capture through text, voice, and files
- life-state interpretation and direction generation
- direction task management and task reflections
- `Backpack` for saved signals and refinement
- `Compass` for deeper life-status diagnostics
- an embedded SurePlay assistant

## Repository Purpose

This repository contains the SurePlay MVP application built with Next.js and Payload, along with the current local AI runtime configuration used for development.
